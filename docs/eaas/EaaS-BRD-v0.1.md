# EaaSGrid EaaS Business Requirements Document

Version: 0.1 | Status: BASELINE / LIVING DOCUMENT

## 1. Purpose

Build a national Nigerian Energy-as-a-Service platform at eaas.xaasgrid.com.
The product shall combine customer acquisition, energy discovery, assessment,
engineering workflow, commercial modelling, service delivery, monitoring,
billing, documentation and customer operations.

## 2. Business principles

1. National by architecture: 36 states plus FCT from the beginning.
2. Phased execution: initial commercial focus may begin in Oyo, Lagos and FCT.
3. One XaaSGrid identity for internal applications with RBAC; no shared passwords.
4. One-person operating model: automation reduces repetitive operational work.
5. Kaizen: measure, improve, document, standardise and automate continuously.
6. Human authority remains over consequential engineering, financial and contractual decisions.
7. Evidence-driven delivery: requirements map to tests, UAT and acceptance evidence.

## 3. Primary users

- Prospective business/customer
- XaaSGrid sales/operator
- Energy assessor/engineer
- Installer/field partner
- Finance/commercial operator
- Customer administrator
- XaaSGrid platform administrator

## 4. Core capabilities

- Public EaaS website and state/FCT coverage
- Energy assessment request and qualification
- Prospect CRM and sales pipeline
- Preliminary load and system modelling
- Asset/BOQ catalogue
- EaaS pricing and financial model
- Proposal and contract workflow
- Site survey, installation and commissioning workflow
- IoT/energy monitoring integration layer
- Subscription billing and payments
- Customer portal
- Living BRD, functional documentation and UAT evidence
- Downloadable documentation packages
